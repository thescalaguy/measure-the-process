CONSULTANCY

We are a boutique software management consultancy in Mumbai, India. We help growing startups iteratively improve by understanding how their processes affect engineers, rather than measuring engineers themselves.

We consult a small number of companies.

We understand them completely.

We stay with them for years.

That is the entire idea.

02

PHILOSOPHY

Measure the process, not the engineer. We believe every growing startup should measure how their engineers experience the organization rather than putting them under the microscope. This helps startups preserve trust while improving in small, incremental steps.

03

APPROACH

STEP 01

Measure

We begin by establishing a baseline for where the organization stands. This helps us understand how engineers experience the organization they work with.

STEP 02

Observe

We observe the trends in the underlying data. We look for signals that indicate where a particular aspect of the process may need improvement.

STEP 03

Diagnose & Intervene

We dig deeper and look for the causes that may lead to those signals. Once we have identified plausible causes, we suggest ways to mitigate them.

STEP 04

Re-Measure

We measure again to see how aspects of work have improved over time. We help create a loop where the organization improves iteratively over time.
